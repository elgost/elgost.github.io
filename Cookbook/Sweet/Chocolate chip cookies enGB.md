# Chocolate chip cookies

## Ingredients
- 2 1/4 Cups flour
- 1 Tsp baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup butter
- 1 cup butter
- 2/3 cup peanut butter
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 cup packed brwon sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla
- 12 ounces semi sweet chocolate chips

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 325 degrees
2. Combine flour, bakings soda and salt in a small bowl
3. Combine butter, peanut butter, sugar and brown sugar in a different bowl. Beat until fluffy (ca 3 min)
4. Add eggs and vanilla.
5. Add flour mixture to butter mixture
6. Stir until blended.
7. Add the chocolate chips
8. Divide into flat coockies on baking paper.
9. Bake until lightly browned around the edges and on top.
10. Remove pan to a baking rack to cool for 3 minutes. Then remove cookies with a flat spatula and cool completely.
